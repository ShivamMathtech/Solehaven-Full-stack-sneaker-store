# Asset sources

The uploaded interface was used as a visual reference for orange accents, light backgrounds, rounded cards, and the shopping flows. It is not redistributed as application content.

Shoe photographs were downloaded from Unsplash and bundled in `public/images/` so the app has no live image-host dependency. They are illustrative, not a verified retailer catalog; concept product names, prices, and colorways may differ from a photographed commercial product. The app is not affiliated with Nike, Puma, or Unsplash.
